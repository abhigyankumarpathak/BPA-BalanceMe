Navigation Options

Five layout prototypes with placeholder sections. Open each one, click around, and resize the window to check mobile.

1

Top Bar

Classic sticky horizontal bar with a call-to-action button. Becomes a hamburger dropdown on mobile.

 2

Sidebar

Fixed left panel with grouped links, like a guide or handbook. Slides out as a drawer on mobile.

 3

One-Page Scroll

Every section on one long page, with side dots that track where you are and a reading progress bar.

 4

Bottom Tabs

Floating tab bar that matches the mobile app's feel. Turns into a native-style tab bar on phones.

 5

Full-Screen Menu

Clean, minimal header. One menu button opens a large full-screen list of sections.
